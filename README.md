# desktop-rme-clinic-system

## Overview
Desktop-based Electronic Medical Record (EMR/RME) system for clinic operations, including patient registration, medical examination, pharmacy, cashier, reporting, and financial management.

## Features
- Patient Registration
- Medical Examination
- Electronic Medical Record (EMR)
- Diagnosis Management
- Pharmacy & Prescription
- Cashier & Billing
- Financial Reporting
- Doctor Service Management
  - Printable Medical Reports

## System Architecture

This application uses a modular desktop-based architecture where each clinic service is separated into functional modules.

### Main Modules

- Front Office
  - Patient registration
  - Queue management
  - Medical card printing

- Medical Examination
  - Diagnosis input
  - Doctor examination
  - Medical records
  - Treatment management

- Pharmacy
  - Prescription management
  - Drug stock control
  - Drug sales

- Finance
  - Billing transaction
  - Payment management
  - Cash flow reporting

- Reporting
  - Medical reports
  - Financial reports
  - Operational statistics

### Database

- MySQL relational database
- Centralized patient and transaction data storage

### Reporting Engine

- JasperReport used for printable invoices and reports.

## Medical Workflow

The medical workflow in this system follows the real clinic operational process.
Patient Registration
        ↓
Medical Examination
        ↓
Diagnosis & Treatment
        ↓
Prescription & Pharmacy
        ↓
Billing & Payment
        ↓
Reporting

### Workflow Process

1. Patient Registration
   - Patient identity input
   - Medical record number generation

2. Queue & Examination
   - Doctor assignment
   - Complaint recording
   - Diagnosis input

3. Treatment & Prescription
   - Medical treatment entry
   - Drug prescription management

4. Billing & Payment
   - Treatment cost calculation
   - Drug payment
   - Cashier transaction

5. Reporting & Record Storage
   - Medical history storage
   - Financial reporting
